Pool Consulting & Expert Witness Services

Aquatic systems involve complex dynamics of structural engineering, mechanical filtration, hydraulics, and volatile chemistry. Construction failures or maintenance oversights can rapidly scale into million-dollar liabilities.

Randy Beard provides peerless forensic investigations, code interpretations, and clear, credible testimony for law firms, developers, insurers, and property owners.

"In mediation or cross-examination, success depends on data-driven clarity. We isolate the structural failure or safety non-compliance with absolute forensic accuracy."

Pool Restoration & Preservation Services

Below our primary consulting work, we provide Orange County homeowners and property managers with hands-on, specialist restoration and management services to keep swimming pools structurally sound and visually immaculate.

Waterline Tile Cleaning & Bead Blasting

Over time, pool water evaporates, leaving behind stubborn white calcium scale along the waterline. We utilize advanced, low-pressure glass bead blasting technology to safely blast away calcium deposits and restore the original luster of tile and stone without scratching or etching the underlying surface.
